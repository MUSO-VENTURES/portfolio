About Me

Passionate motion designer crafting visual stories that captivate and inspire

Lead Motion Designer

With over 25 years of experience in motion design and animation, I've led motion initiatives for enterprise, SaaS, and entertainment brands including Clear Digital, Capcom, and Aeria Games. My years at Apple and Facebook analyzing visual data gave me the ability to use that insight in design centered around user experiences. Currently working as Lead Motion Designer at Clear Digital in Silicon Valley, I specialize in translating complex ideas into clear, engaging motion systems across web, product UI, and marketing campaigns.

I'm passionate about mentoring teams, collaborating with designers and developers, and adapting to emerging technology and trends in the motion design landscape. Whether it's exploring AI-powered workflows, real-time rendering, or interactive animation techniques, I embrace new tools and methodologies to stay ahead of the curve. My approach combines technical expertise with strategic thinking to deliver scalable, polished motion solutions. From storyboarding to final delivery, I bring creativity, precision, and a forward-thinking approach to every project.
